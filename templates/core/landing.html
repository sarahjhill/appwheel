{# ---------- Getting apps in ---------- #}

Four ways to add your apps

You don't have to type it all in. Pick whichever way is easiest for you.

  • Upload a bank statement

    A PDF or CSV from your banking app. We find the payments that repeat, then throw the file away.

  • Take a screenshot

    Of the Subscriptions page on your iPhone or Android. AppWheel reads it for you.

  • Paste an email

    Copy a receipt or "thanks for subscribing" email and paste it in.

  • Choose from a list

    Search over 50 popular apps. Pick your plan and the price fills itself in.

{# ---------- The lights ---------- #}

One look tells you what needs doing

Every app gets a light, always with a symbol as well as a colour.

  • ✓ Good value

    Spotify, £11.99 a month. You use it every day and there's nothing cheaper for you.

  • ! Could cost less

    Netflix, £12.99 a month. The plan with adverts would save you £60 a year.

  • ✕ Needs action

    Gym, £24.99 a month. You said you never go. Cancelling saves £299.88 a year.

When you're ready to cancel, Cancel with help shows you the steps for how you pay, reminds you to check you weren't charged again, and writes the refund request for you if you were.

{# ---------- Care diary ---------- #}

If you get care at home, keep a record that counts

Each day, answer four quick questions: did your carer arrive on time, were you treated with kindness and respect, did you get your medication, and did you get help with food, washing and dressing.

If something isn't right, AppWheel tells you who to go to, in order: the care company, your council, the care regulator and the ombudsman. Download your diary as a tidy PDF to send to whoever you choose.

Your diary is private. AppWheel never sends it to anyone. You decide who sees it.

Help when you need it

Short guides for Facebook, Instagram, TikTok, WhatsApp, X and Snapchat: block someone, make posts private, report a scam, and a monthly privacy check-up.

Made to work the way you do

AppWheel works with screen readers, a keyboard or switch, and text zoomed right up. Colours always come with symbols. Nothing moves unless you ask it to, and it stays still if you've turned off motion on your device. If anything gets in your way, tell us and we'll fix it.
