Care diary

Keep a simple daily record of the care you get at home.

Each day, answer four quick questions: was your carer on time, were you treated with kindness, did you get your medication, and did you get help with food, washing and dressing. Add a rating and any notes.

If something goes wrong, your diary shows exactly what happened and when, and AppWheel tells you who to go to.

Before you start

  • Your diary is about your health and care, so it's private and only you can see it.
  • AppWheel never sends it to anyone. You decide who to share it with.
  • You can delete any entry, or your whole diary, at any time.
