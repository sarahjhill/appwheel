Not happy with your care?

If someone is in danger right now, call 999.

Worried about abuse or neglect? Contact your council's adult safeguarding team. They can act quickly. Find your council.

Otherwise, these are the usual steps, in order:

  1. Talk to the care company. Ask for the manager. Say what happened and when. Your diary shows the dates.
  2. Contact your council's adult social care team, especially if the council arranges or pays for your care.
  3. Tell the care regulator. In Wales: Care Inspectorate Wales. In England: Care Quality Commission.
  4. Still not sorted after complaining? Go to the ombudsman. In Wales: Public Services Ombudsman for Wales. In England: Local Government and Social Care Ombudsman.
