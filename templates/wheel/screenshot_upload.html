How to take the screenshot

iPhone: open Settings, tap your name at the top, then Subscriptions. Take a screenshot by pressing the side button and volume up together.

Android: open the Play Store, tap your picture at the top, then Payments and subscriptions, then Subscriptions. Take a screenshot by pressing power and volume down together.

You can also screenshot a bill or a "thanks for subscribing" email.

Your screenshots stay private

  • Your picture is sent securely to our AI reader (Claude, made by Anthropic) so it can read the details. It is not used to train AI.
  • AppWheel never saves your picture.
  • Only the subscriptions you tick on the next page are added.
