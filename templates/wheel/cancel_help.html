How do you pay for {{ subscription.name }}?

Choose the one that matches. If you're not sure, check your bank statement: payments through Apple say "APPLE.COM/BILL" and Google ones say "GOOGLE".

Through my iPhone or iPad (Apple)
  1. Open the Settings app.
  2. Tap your name at the top.
  3. Tap Subscriptions.
  4. Tap {{ subscription.name }}.
  5. Tap Cancel Subscription and confirm.
Through Google Play (Android)
  1. Open the Play Store app.
  2. Tap your picture at the top right.
  3. Tap Payments and subscriptions, then Subscriptions.
  4. Tap {{ subscription.name }}, then Cancel subscription.
On {{ subscription.name }}'s own website or app
  1. Sign in on {{ subscription.name }}'s official website or app. Type the address yourself rather than using a link in an email or advert.
  2. Go to Account or Settings.
  3. Look for Membership, Plan, Subscription or Billing.
  4. Choose Cancel. Keep the confirmation email.
By Direct Debit, or it's a phone, TV or broadband contract
  1. Contact {{ subscription.name }} and ask to cancel. Contracts can have a minimum term or a notice period.
  2. Ask them to confirm the cancellation in writing or by email.
  3. Only cancel the Direct Debit with your bank once they've confirmed. Cancelling the Direct Debit on its own doesn't end a contract.
